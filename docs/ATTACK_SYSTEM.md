# Three-slot crab attacks — rules version 2

The beach arena, trainer, gear and integer combat calculations remain in place. Each fighter snapshots exactly three moves on Start Battle. No mid-match equipment or move edit changes that snapshot.

## Two separate unlock paths

- Main attacks unlock permanently through **crab battle level**. `level = 1 + floor(battleXP / 100)`. Completed wins grant 40 battle XP, losses 15 and exits 0. Selecting a newly unlocked move is explicit; unlocking never replaces the current main attack.
- Equipment specials require the relevant **currently equipped owned collectible**, at any crab level. Discovery alone adds gear to inventory, not to the active move set. With multiple eligible pieces, choose one special in Workshop → Moves.
- The existing **explorer level** remains derived from optional self-reported cleanup XP. Battle rewards do not add cleanup credit. Neither level grants additional HP/ATK/DEF/SPD; existing gear bonuses remain the stat source.
- Defend always occupies slot 2. If there are no gear specials, slot 3 is explicitly labeled **Basic Splash**, retaining the former Tidal Slam power/cooldown. If a selected special becomes ineligible, the first eligible special in registry order replaces it, or Basic Splash if none remain. Re-equipping gear does not resurrect an invalid old selection.

## Exact combat semantics

- Per-hit damage is `max(1, floor(power + max(0, ATK − attackReduction) + speedScale × SPD − target.DEF / 2))`. Multiply by hit count. Defend halves the complete action, rounding up once. Pinch and Basic Splash preserve their existing damage formula.
- Defend lasts until the next incoming damaging action and never stacks. Counter replaces Defend, and Defend replaces a pending counter. The counter fires once if the defender survives, then clears. Its fixed 6 reaction damage ignores defense/guard and cannot trigger another counter. A counter KO ends the match immediately; a defender knocked out by the initial hit cannot counter.
- Bag Snare refreshes a single ATK reduction, never stacks. The affected fighter loses one duration at the end of each own turn, including Defend. Base stats are unchanged. Counter's fixed reaction damage is unaffected.
- Each move has its own cooldown. Using a cooldown-2 move blocks it on the next two own turns; enemy turns do not tick it. Cooldowns are checked before resolving. Non-equipped actions, wrong actors, completed matches and cooling moves are rejected by the pure resolver.
- CPU keeps its authored Can Shield and deterministic choices. It learns the highest main attack at the player's crab level (up to the level-12 move); HP and gear stats remain unchanged. Its three slots follow the same eligibility rules. No fabricated human opponent.

## Persistence and boundaries

IndexedDB `pixelogy-v4` upgrades additively from version 3 to **4**, adding `battleProgression` and `battleRewards`. The existing collectibles, artwork, cleanup records, privacy preferences, profile and loadout stores remain intact. Legacy players begin at crab level 1 with Pinch Attack; eligible specials derive from their existing equipped gear. No old practice XP is invented.

`readGame` repairs invalid main/special selections, derives unlocked main IDs from monotonic battle XP, and saves them. Equipment updates reconcile special eligibility in the same transaction. `saveMoveSelection` re-reads current owned equipment and progression inside a transaction before accepting a choice. A completed-match reward and XP/unlock update are one transaction keyed by match ID, preventing duplicate rewards from retries or concurrent calls. Results show pending/save failure with retry; replay waits for a successful save. Exiting or reloading an unfinished match earns nothing.

There are no existing Supabase tables or authentication in this repository. This is local progression, not server-authoritative anti-cheat. The pure eligibility functions and resolver have no browser or database dependencies; a future server must load trusted ownership/progression and validate them before using the engine. Never trust submitted local XP, snapshots or battle results for public rankings or PvP.
