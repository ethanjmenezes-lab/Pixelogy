# Pixelogy cinematic artwork

ShotStage.tsx, RegionalShots.ts, StoryArt.tsx and cinematic.css contain original code-generated Pixelogy artwork and animation. They contain no downloaded, traced or licensed third-party character art. Existing PlayerAvatar and Crab compositions remain original Pixelogy assets.

Audio is a quiet, original synthesized square-wave motif triggered only after Unmute. There are no sampled recordings or voice performances. It is an initial sound treatment, not a full recreation of every sound-design cue in the script.
